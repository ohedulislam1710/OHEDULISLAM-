About Me

Still learning, still building.

My name is OHEDULISLAM, and I work as a Creative Freelancer & Digital Marketer. My journey into digital and creative work began in 2024 — not with a big company behind me, but with a simple wish to learn, to build, and to create an identity of my own.

I started with Graphic Design, learning how to present an idea clearly and make it visually compelling. From there, my interests grew into Digital Marketing, Web Design & Development, WordPress, E-commerce, Video Editing, and Social Media Management.

I believe the best way to learn is by building real projects — not just watching tutorials. Every project I've worked on, from e-commerce experiences to entertainment concepts, has taught me something about structure, design, and how a digital product should feel to the people using it.

I hold a Graphic Design certificate from Mentors IT Academy — a reminder of the time I've invested in this craft. But for me, a certificate is not a finish line; it's where the responsibility to keep improving actually begins.

Today, I want to work closely with local business clients, because I believe every small business deserves a digital identity as good as any large one.

My Story

The story is still being written.

My name is OHEDULISLAM. My story may not have started with a big organization or a remarkable achievement. It started in a much simpler way — with the desire to learn, the curiosity to create something new, and a dream to build an identity of my own.

My creative and digital journey began in 2024. At that time, I did not know everything. But I knew one thing — I did not want to simply watch other people create. I wanted to create something myself. And that is where my journey of learning began.

I started with Graphic Design. I gradually learned how an idea can be presented beautifully, how an ordinary concept can become visually engaging, and how design can communicate something without saying too much. Along the way, I realized that good design is not only about looking beautiful — it is about having a purpose, communicating clearly, and creating something that people can understand and remember.

Then my interests began to grow. I started learning and working with Digital Marketing, Web Design/Web Development, WordPress, E-commerce, Video Editing, and Social Media Management.

The journey from one skill to another was not always easy. Many things did not work perfectly the first time. I faced problems, had to learn again, and sometimes had to start over. But every time I faced a problem, I tried to understand it, fix it, and move forward.

For me, one of the most important parts of learning has always been learning by doing. Instead of only watching tutorials or looking at other people's work, I started creating my own projects. Every project taught me something new — how to organize an idea, how to structure a website, how design and functionality can work together, and how a digital product can be presented professionally.

During this journey, several projects became especially meaningful to me. SUMU SHOP became one of my projects focused on e-commerce and digital experience — I explored product presentation, browsing experience, and the overall shopping flow. OKEJO was a different kind of digital entertainment concept, through which I explored content, interaction, and user experience. And through Mayer Doya Bostraloy (মায়ের দোয়া বস্ত্রালয়), I worked with the idea of clothing and e-commerce presentation.

For me, these projects are more than just names on a portfolio. They are part of my learning journey. Every time I complete a project, I feel that I know a little more than I did before — and that is one of the things I value most.

As part of my learning journey, I also received a Graphic Design Certificate from Mentors IT Academy. To me, a certificate is more than just a piece of paper — it represents the time I invested in learning and improving a skill. But I also believe that receiving a certificate does not mean that learning is finished. Instead, it creates a responsibility to become even better.

Today, I am building myself as a Creative Freelancer & Digital Marketer. My work and learning cover Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, Web, WordPress, E-commerce, Video Editing, and Social Media Management. I especially want to work with local business clients. I believe that every business, even a small one, deserves a strong digital identity. Good design, a professional website, and the right digital presentation can help a business communicate more confidently and build trust with its audience.

My goal is not simply to work as a freelancer. I want to build a strong personal brand of my own. I want my work to communicate who I am before I even introduce myself. I am still learning, but I am serious about what I do. I am not afraid to create something new. When something goes wrong, I want to learn from it. And with every new project, I want to become better than I was before.

My journey is still young. There is still so much to learn, many more projects to create, many mistakes to make, and many lessons to discover. I do not know exactly where I will be a few years from now. But I know one thing — I do not want to stop.

The journey that began in 2024 is still continuing. Today, I know a little more than I did yesterday. And tomorrow, I want to become better than I am today. I am still learning. I am still creating. I am still building my identity. And perhaps that is the most beautiful part of my journey.

"The story is still being written."
OHEDULISLAM
